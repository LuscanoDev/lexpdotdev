FAQ

-> what does lexpdev mean?

l means lucas, exp means experiment and dev means developer. no, i'm not good with names... you can call me lexp or just lex.

-> how do you know how to do this??

a lot of testing, a lot of inspiration, a lot of researching (and a lot of screen time)!! i didn't take any courses, i learned everything from scratch and it ended up like this (^_^;)

-> why don't you like social networks?

both because of the algorithms that trap you inside them, and because of the absurd amount of AI on them.

-> where do you host this?

i host the website on Github Pages, and the projects that have a backend i host on a raspberry pi 4.

-> why is X project offline?

maybe i'm out of power, maybe something went very wrong... let me know!!

-> how did you make these banners?

i pick several images i took or found online and edit them in gimp.

-> what are your favorite games?

i don’t play that much, but my favorite games right now are Hatsune Miku Project Diva and Taiko no Tatsujin. other kinds of games usually don’t catch my attention for some reason :p

-> what are these “buttons”?

they’re small images or gifs that lead to another website i liked, making a big connection with other sites in the indieweb.

-> how did you put the music you're listening to on the side of the page?

using a script that gets the music from my lastfm profile and puts it on my site. thanks to vojislav for making the code and yuuinet for fixing a few things!!

-> how can i donate some money to you?

first of all, thank you for liking my site so much!! currently, i don’t have any place to receive donations, but if you REALLY want to, contact me.
